## Evidence notes

- Is it contemporaneous with Freeze?
- Did the Focal Actor plausibly have access to this information?
- Is it direct evidence, corroboration, or interpretation?
- Are there translation / edition / OCR / transcription risks?

## Packet use

- Reader-visible before Freeze?
- Research-only before Freeze?
- Reveal-only?
- If retrospective, how will the time identity be disclosed?

## Citation / locator

Record page, image, box/folder, item ID, timestamp or section as precisely as available.

## Copyright / storage note

Do not copy full copyrighted works into the repository unless clearly permitted. Prefer bibliographic data, stable links, short quotations within fair-use limits, and research notes.
