## Core question

> On 1918-10-07, should Starkloff move from case reporting / patient isolation / limited measures to actively pushing citywide closure of schools, places of amusement and public gatherings?

## Freeze

Operational Freeze: **1918-10-07 cross-agency meeting, before the public commitment to the broad closure route is complete.**

The project does not claim to know the precise private moment when Starkloff changed his mind.
